Python was developed by Guido van Rossum in 1989. He began the project with inspiration from the ABC language in which he would fix some of the limitations that said language contained. It was nothing more than a hobby, named after a popular BBC series called "Monty Python's Flying Circus" but it quickly became a popular language for its ease of use and versatility.
